## Method

This file is a compact scouting layer, not a full 26-player roster database. It identifies the highest-impact players and current-form storylines for each qualified team.

Ranking tiers:

- Global elite: credible top-25 tournament player, Ballon d'Or-level profile, or elite market-value player.
- Continental elite: among the strongest players in their confederation or position group.
- Team star: central player for the national team, usually first-name-on-the-teamsheet level.
- Form riser: player whose recent club or qualifying form makes them especially relevant.
- Veteran anchor: high-experience player whose tactical value is leadership, control, or game management.

## Current-Performance Watch Items

- Spain, England, France, Brazil, Germany, Portugal, Netherlands, and Argentina have the deepest concentration of global elite players and are the teams where omissions, role choices, and tactical fit matter almost as much as the star names.
- Norway are unusually star-heavy for a country outside the traditional World Cup favorites because Haaland and Odegaard give them elite attacking gravity.
- Sweden are a dangerous qualifier despite a poor group-stage qualifying record because their forward line is stronger than their table position suggested.
- Ecuador, Morocco, Uruguay, Japan, and Senegal rate as high-upside non-favorites because they combine strong recent qualifying/team structure with several players performing in top European leagues.
- Haiti, Curacao, Cape Verde, Uzbekistan, and Jordan are debut/return-story teams where one or two stars carrying form into the first match could reshape their group.
